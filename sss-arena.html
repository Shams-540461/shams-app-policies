Privacy Policy for SSS Arena

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

Shams Smart Services built SSS Arena as a free mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use SSS Arena.

1. Information We Collect

SSS Arena does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information.

The game does not intentionally collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

SSS Arena may store limited game information locally on your device, including:

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to Shams Smart Services or any external server. You can remove this locally stored information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

SSS Arena currently does not display third-party advertisements and does not use third-party analytics or tracking services.

If advertising, analytics, online services, or additional data-related features are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

4. Third-Party Platforms

SSS Arena is distributed through Google Play. Google may process certain information related to downloading, installing, security, diagnostics, or using Google Play services according to Google's own Privacy Policy. Such processing is controlled by Google and not by SSS Arena.

5. Children's Privacy

SSS Arena is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

6. Data Security

Because SSS Arena does not intentionally collect or transmit personal user data to our servers, no personal user database is maintained by Shams Smart Services for this game.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, its features, or applicable legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
