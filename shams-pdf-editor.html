Privacy Policy for Shams PDF Editor

Effective Date: October 4, 2026
Provider: Shams Smart Services
Android Package: com.shams.pdfeditor

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Shams PDF Editor on Android or through its web editor.

1. Files and online text editing

The editor accesses PDFs and images that you choose for editing. When you use online original-text selection or replacement, the complete current PDF is sent to our processing service, together with the selection details, replacement text and formatting options needed for the operation. This may include personal information contained in your document.

The service uses this information to identify text, carry out your requested edit, validate the result and return the response. Online original-text editing requires an internet connection.

2. Processing and document storage

Our PDF editing backend is hosted on Render in Oregon, United States, and uses the Syncfusion PDF library. Submitted PDFs are processed in server memory. The editing service does not maintain a document archive or intentionally save uploaded or edited PDFs to a file or database. Temporary processing objects are released after the operation; this is not a promise of immediate secure erasure of every memory copy.

PDFs you save or download remain in the location you choose. You can delete those copies using your device or storage provider.

3. Technical information and logs

Hosting providers process technical information needed to operate the service, such as connection information, IP addresses and request metadata. Unexpected processing errors are logged for troubleshooting. These error details may include document-related information supplied by the PDF library.

Render dashboard runtime logs on our current Hobby workspace have a seven-day retention period. This period describes dashboard runtime logs, not a PDF storage period or a guarantee covering every infrastructure record held by service providers.

4. Service providers and security

Render provides backend hosting, and GitHub Pages hosts the web editor. Processing may occur outside your country. Syncfusion is used as a PDF processing library on our backend; the editing implementation does not separately upload your PDF to a Syncfusion processing service.

The public editing endpoint uses HTTPS, and the API requests that responses not be cached. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

5. Advertising

The current version of Shams PDF Editor does not display advertisements. If advertising is added in a later version, this policy and the relevant app disclosures will be updated to explain the resulting data practices.

6. Your choices and support

You can choose not to use online original-text selection or replacement if you do not want the PDF sent to the editing backend. Only submit documents you are authorized to process.

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send so that we can respond. Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of support information. This policy does not specify a fixed automatic deletion deadline for support correspondence. Please avoid emailing sensitive documents unless necessary.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The updated policy will be published with a revised effective date.

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@shamswmalik.com and mention Shams PDF Editor.
