Privacy Policy – My Expense

Effective date: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how My Expense (the “App”), developed by Shams Smart Services, handles information when you use its expense-management features, Nadia assistant, and Black Blast Squad game.

1. Information stored on your device

In the current version, expense and income records that you enter—such as amounts, dates, categories, descriptions, and related app preferences—are stored locally on your device. The App does not currently provide an online database, user-account synchronization, or cloud backup for these records.

Local records may be permanently lost if you clear the App's storage, reset your device, or uninstall the App. Please keep your own backup of any information you need to retain.

2. Nadia AI assistant

Nadia uses Google's Gemini AI service to generate responses. When you send a message to Nadia, the message and recent conversation messages may be sent through our backend server to Google Gemini.

When the App provides financial context to Nadia, relevant expense and income information, categories, budgets, currency preferences, or other information included in that context may also be transmitted to our backend and Google Gemini so Nadia can answer your question. Therefore, financial information you enter in the App may leave your device when you use Nadia.

Please do not send passwords, PINs, one-time passcodes, banking credentials, or other highly sensitive information to Nadia. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy information, including Google's Privacy Policy and the terms applicable to Google AI services.

3. Backend processing and retention

Our backend processes Nadia requests to obtain AI responses. The current backend code does not intentionally save expense records or Nadia conversations to an application database. However, the backend hosting provider may process technical request information, such as connection and diagnostic logs, as part of operating and securing the service. We do not control Google's or hosting providers' independent retention practices.

4. Game feature

Black Blast Squad provides gameplay functionality. Do not enter personal, financial, or confidential information into the game.

5. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with Google Gemini when needed to answer Nadia requests, including relevant financial context supplied by the App. Technical information may also be processed by the backend hosting provider to operate the service.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information processed by the App and its backend. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your choices and deletion

You can manage or delete expense records within the App where those controls are available. Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it removes locally stored records from that installation. If you have a privacy question or request concerning information processed through Nadia, contact us using the details below. Information already sent to Google or the hosting provider may remain subject to their applicable retention and deletion policies.

8. Children's privacy

My Expense is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Nadia, please contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this same URL, with its effective date updated when appropriate.
